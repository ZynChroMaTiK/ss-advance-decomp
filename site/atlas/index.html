SECRET COVERAGE

All 16 levels and every decoded room are revealed. Five documented hidden pickups/rooms are pinned in levels 1–5. The cited guides list no secrets for levels 7, 8, 10 and 11. Secret classification is unverified for levels 6, 9, 12 and the multiplayer maps.

No dedicated ROM secret flag has been established. Pins combine guide descriptions with exact ROM positions; they do not prove exhaustive discovery of every easter egg. Each pin links to its identification source.

LOCATION & TIMING

Axes: +Y upward, +X right. Intel shows initial floor and ceiling heights. Door locks are the initial state; scripts can change them. Repeated identical spawns share an icon, with all instances retained in the details and exported data.
