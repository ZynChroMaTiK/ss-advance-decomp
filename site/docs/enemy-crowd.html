PC enemy crowd movement

The playable frontend enables local body sliding for CD and CE..D8 walking controllers. A blocked enemy moves along the blocking enemy's square collision face, retaining its chosen side across frames so it can get around the body instead of steering immediately back into it. Centered head-on pairs choose opposite world directions. If a wall blocks that side, the other side is tried. The original navigation, turning, speed, attack logic and Time Strike freeze gate remain in place. This is a requested PC behavior extension, not original ROM behavior or a new matching function.

ssa_enemy_walk_world_sliding uses the recovered walking routine's existing movement callback. The movement-call counter and vertical physics still run once per walk, including blocked walks. Horizontal displacement is split into steps of at most four units per axis. A lateral step uses the larger requested component, so its length cannot exceed that requested step's length. Every candidate goes through world entity contacts, static walls, sector clearance, steps and normal spatial unlink/relink. It moves only the current enemy and does not push other bodies, clear collision flags, or exclude a neighbor.

Already overlapping enemy pairs can move gradually apart. The PC query permits this only when they were already colliding, neither absolute separation shrinks, at least one grows, and neither center is crossed. Other bodies are checked independently: a previously clear pair cannot gain overlap under this exception. The exception does not apply to players. Player blockages retain the original blocked result for contact attacks; failed side probes restore the original enemy blocker instead of leaving a later probe's wall/player result behind. For Prion melee, crowd movement additionally records the first real enemy contact before trying sidesteps. This transient contact resets on every move; it is distinct from the persistent side-preference blocker. Combat consumes it within the same controller call, allowing melee when either body is converted even if the attacker subsequently slides successfully. It is never reused as a target on a later frame or dereferenced after actor retirement.

The PC query extends entity-cell searches by128 units to cover all original enemy radii, including Gunrilla's90 units across cell boundaries. Original ssa_world_query/try_move retain their32-unit search margin and exact recovered contact behavior. The world structure and original API layouts are unchanged. The crowd API's coverage contract is original enemy radii up to128 units.

Zero-initialize one SsaEnemyCrowd per enemy lifetime. Its cached blocker is an identity for the side preference only and is never dereferenced. Generic runtime bindings reset this state on slot reuse; CD initialization clears its own state. crowd_slide defaults off in both runtime adapters. The native frontend enables the generic adapter and game.enemy_crowd_slide, which propagates to newly bound CD actors. Shared recovered controller/motion entry points stay unchanged. State/world query guards reject reentry; callbacks must honor existing world ownership and synchronous-special contracts. Stable storage remains required.

PC player clearance

Campaign probing exposed the same32-unit broadphase limit in player movement. A Gunrilla's90-unit radius can overlap Sam before its center enters that search. Once the overlap is detected, the original predicate can reject retreat as well. ssa_world_try_move_player searches128 units for enemy centers and permits only monotonic separation of an already overlapping player/enemy pair: neither axis distance may decrease, at least one must increase, and centers cannot cross. New contacts, walls, floor/ceiling checks and spatial relinking remain active. The enemy is never moved or made nonsolid.

Only CD..D8 bodies participate in the additional cells. In particular, E1 pickups retain their original cell-based touch range and script timing. The original query, try_move, horizontal and player-motion entry points retain their ROM behavior. ssa_world_horizontal_step_player and ssa_player_motion_world_step_pc bind the policy to the existing PC player runtime without changing public structure layouts.

The12 focused scenarios now include reproducing the missed Gunrilla contact, blocked approaches, gradual retreat, a second enemy obstructing retreat, center crossing, wall limits, unchanged pickup coverage and invalid/reentrant calls. An isolated player-runtime fixture runs16 movement/jump/H frames out of a controlled overlapping spawn with presentation/hazard boundaries controlled; the separate512-frame full-game fixture still runs real native AI. GCC ASan/UBSan/leaks and MSVC agree on20,456 checks. Original world tests and 8,672 original player-motion comparisons also pass. Windows gameplay smoke, both startup renderers and the normal5110-frame first-level completion pass.
