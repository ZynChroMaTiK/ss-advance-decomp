Unlimited ranged attacks

The native playable build removes weapon-distance limits from ranged hitscan attacks, including shotgun side pellets. Player projectiles continue until an impact instead of expiring after480 ticks. The chainsaw now uses the requested 192-unit PC reach (the original ROM table is96), with a finite trace; damage, ammo consumption, cadence, spread, blast radii and enemy-projectile expiry are unchanged. Existing unfinished special-weapon behavior remains outside this range change.

SsaWeaponRuntime.unlimited_ranged and SsaProjectileRuntime.unlimited_player_range enable this PC policy in ssa_native_game_init. Both default to zero for original-behavior integrations. The ROM rules and original controller, hit-query and projectile-motion APIs retain their recovered behavior. No changes are made to the ROM or matching export.

ssa_weapon_hit_query_unlimited advances512-unit segments until the first entity, blocking wall, floor, ceiling or world boundary. This segment size is a numerical step, not a maximum distance: the original traversal visits at most64 cells per query, and long rays must not silently stop collecting geometry. Every segment preserves the actual source identity and resets its own intercept storage. Coordinate-boundary checks prevent fixed-point wrap. Queries do not apply damage or invoke gameplay callbacks.

Manual aim queries use the extended ray. Original autoaim retains its nearby vertical selection and can acquire distant targets on the level ray. Hitscan events preserve original nearby impacts and extend clear rays beyond the former cutoff; actual combat still performs impact callbacks, damage and hit credit. Near-muzzle projectile fractions remain relative to the controller's original range so distant contacts do not pull a projectile backward on creation.

ssa_projectile_motion_unlimited removes age expiry only for player-owned shots and saturates their age at480, preventing later16-bit timer wrap from resetting owner-clearance behavior. Collision, bounce, penetration and impact processing still run. Original enemy motion and effect timers are retained.

The third-person camera uses the same extended sight ray for all ten selections, including the chainsaw. Weapon switching therefore preserves the previous camera fix and converges on distant ranged contacts.
