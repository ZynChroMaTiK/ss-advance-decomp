PC alternate weapons

Options > Extras > Alternate weapons enables dual Colts and the requested double-shotgun pellet pattern.

The separate Extras > Weapon bobbing toggle defaults Off and works with all ten weapons, including both Colt hands. It offsets weapon render copies and fixed-screen muzzle flashes together along an arch spanning0..6 GBA pixels to the right and0..2 pixels down, scaled by movement amplitude. Both Colts and their flashes share the same offset and move in parallel. The phase is exactly the recovered footstep phase (camera counter times1872): sound13 lands bottom left, sound14 bottom right, and the weapon rises to the normal baseline between steps. Jump height adds up to4px of dip, including stationary jumps, returning on landing. The baseline never moves upward past the art's original crop. Original weapon and muzzle-flash sizes are preserved. Original positions are the upper-left bounds of the movement; both sprites only shift further right/down. Only the left-hand artwork itself is mirrored. There is no edge filling, resampling or viewport strip extension. At rest offsets are zero, preserving the original image and positions. Weapon bob amplitude reaches full strength at normal straight speed8, rather than diagonal speed: the original camera's squared-speed amplitude was half strength when walking straight, leaving vertical offsets below one native pixel. Only weapon/flash presentation is normalized; camera/footsteps remain the original recovered behavior. All display entry points retain the original strict OBJ cycle budget. In native-aspect software output, bobbing weapon pixels may extend past the centered3:2 canvas. PC-only OAM snapshots separate unshifted original weapon art from the HUD; output-space translation applies bobbing before clipping at the actual viewport edge. No border pixels are invented or stretched. Both Colt hands stay together, while HUD anchors and reticle remain fixed. The reference renderer and exactly3:2 output retain the existing OAM composition path, and zero-offset idle rendering bypasses the separate weapon pass. It does not move the crosshair or modify canonical weapon coordinates, aim, firing, magazines or reload timing. Standing still on the ground has no sway; automap, death and third-person views suppress it. Pause holds the current phase. Repeated rendering never advances it. Software uses the same viewport scale for flash offsets as the weapon; GBA flashes use the corresponding120x120 raster offset. Original actor projection entry points retain zero offsets; the new PC collect entry point mirrors the artwork before applying the shared screen offset. Settings version11 retains the preference introduced in version10; versions1..9 default it Off.

The native PC pickup rules also accept ammo without weapon ownership and owned weapons for their normal ammo grant, with ordinary capacity limits. This applies even when Alternate weapons is Off. The second-Colt acquisition has priority over duplicate-ammo handling, so it still unlocks duals with a full ammo pool. It defaults to Off and persists in Settings v6 (16 bytes, byte9 bit7, existing CRC). Versions1..5 keep their previous preferences and migrate with it Off. Extras retains its grid-aligned labels/values; the new row is at y120 and Back at y152. English, German and French use the original fonts.

With this enabled, a fresh New Game starts Egypt with one Colt and the original Colt pickup's half-capacity ammunition. Password/level-select starts outside the first level upgrade any starting Colt to dual ownership, retaining the original ammunition, other gear and selected weapon ID. If Colts are initially selected, both are drawn at level entry; otherwise selecting Colts chooses the dual pair. The first level keeps its single Colt, and carried/restarted campaign inventory retains the variant actually earned. A Colt pickup while already owning one accepts the second gun, adds the usual pickup ammunition and equips dual Colts. A further Colt is refused as before. The pickup still runs original script triggers, sound, HUD notice, collected statistics and removal. The recovered pickup entry point retains its original behavior; an explicit PC runtime policy permits this one repeat acquisition.

The user chose to retain the current key assignments: Colts are key2, chainsaw key1. Press2 while Colts are requested to toggle single/dual after acquisition. Wheel order is chainsaw, single Colt, dual Colts, then the other original weapon IDs; unowned/empty entries are skipped. Rapid wheel events use the requested variant. Q remembers the previous weapon and variant. Both variants share the original Colt inventory bit, ammunition, HUD and NETRICSA record.

The original Colt controller supplies damage, aim, sounds, ammunition and statistics. With Alternate weapons On, single and dual Colts use the requested0.45second interval per hand. Duals alternate right/left every0.225seconds. Each independently plays the original recoil animation at normal speed. The first shot is right-handed. The shared controller runs two phases per world tick for duals; an explicit firing deadline admits starts. The click edge is delivered once, and other weapons' cooldowns retain one update per world tick. Each fire press queues two alternating shots, including a click released before the next simulation tick. Held fire opens another pair at the next admitted start, completing its second shot after release. Additional click edges queue another pair, so a click during recoil/cadence or reload is retained. Pending shots still obey the magazine, reload and shot deadlines. Changing weapon or variant, death, pause, lost focus and disabled input cancel unstarted shots; an already started shot still commits normally. Single Colts keep one-shot clicks.

Each hand tracks six shots separately from the original infinite reserve ammo/HUD. Automatic reload begins only when the next hand in the alternating sequence is empty. The newly emptied right gun does not block the left gun's sixth shot: full duals fire12shots before reloading. Unequal magazines still respect hand order and never fire an empty gun or skip its turn. Once recoil finishes, the right Colt reloads; the left (duals only) starts0.33seconds later. Each reload takes1.07seconds and the complete dual reload takes1.40seconds. Each hand lowers and rises using original art while the other stays visible. Each hand's reload start plays the original empty-trigger click(sound0x28) as a centered local cue, once per reload phase; it is not looped, attenuated or restarted by pause. Reloading blocks queued weapon changes until completion of both hands, freezes during pause, and resumes held fire with the right hand. The fixed simulation clock uses1/1200second units:540per shot,270between hands, 1284per hand reload,396left-start delay and1680for the pair. reload_end retains the right-hand deadline throughout: phase2 is right-only; phase3 includes overlap and the left-only tail. Each hand refills and finishes its projection independently. Fractional deadlines carry across ticks; rendering frequency does not change the average rate. Each event is quantized to the next60Hz update. The policy is absent when Alternate weapons is Off, preserving original behavior. Press R to reload partially used single or dual Colts manually. This enters the same recoil wait, right/left animation, sound and timing path as automatic reload; both selected hands refill without changing reserve ammo. Full magazines, other weapons, weapon/variant transitions and automap ignore the request. Reload is a one-press action: OS repeats cannot restart it, and pause/focus loss consumes pending requests. It never restarts the level. Re-equipping a gun resets its six-shot counter. A full switch back to Colts refills the right gun and the left if duals are selected; a single-to-dual transition refills only the newly drawn left gun. The right counter is retained through variant changes because that gun remains equipped. Changing single/dual waits for both active hand animations, then moves only the left hand at the original draw/store speed. The right hand stays in place. Quick clicks during this transition remain buffered for its completion. Ordinary changes to another weapon retain the existing full lower/raise path.

Presentation reuses the original Colt art and palette, reflecting its entire sprite across the240px canvas for the left hand. A temporary OAM projection includes both hands without adding gameplay sprite objects. Colt idle uses30 tiles, firing at most45. The two independently animated images occupy reserved weapon banks at tile0 and tile80, after queued sprite uploads have completed, so overlapping recoil frames cannot overwrite each other. No HUD cache allocation is needed. Both renderers, pause and NETRICSA's weapon-only snapshot use the same projection. Left muzzle flashes are mirrored through an explicit PC projection path; original D490/D884 entry points retain their previous semantics. Third-person still hides first-person weapons/flashes.

Value-only campaign carry/restart snapshots include dual ownership, selection, the Extras policy, magazine counts and Q history. Carried counts are checked against the next gun if Colts remain selected; re-equipping them instead refills the guns being drawn. There are no new ROM writes or save-game files. Disabling alternate weapons restores single-Colt selection and original pickup acceptance.

The double shotgun uses14fixed pellets while this option is On: three at vertical+2.58degrees/horizontal-5.14,0,+5.14; four at+0.86/-6.84,-1.72,+1.72,+6.84; three at-0.86/-5.14,0,+5.14; four at-2.58/-6.84,-1.72,+1.72,+6.84. Each pellet contributes5base damage,70if all hit the same enemy. The view-local pattern rotates around actual yaw/free-aim pitch or the selected GBA auto-aim slope, using the original math tables. Angle rounding is within one turn/65536. Both modes use explicit full-precision rays; the original GBA heading-override quirk remains only with the option Off. All pellets retain unlimited PC range, wall/floor/ceiling stops, impact effects and the original two-shell consumption, single trigger statistic and firing animation/sound.

Its alternate firing interval is1.75seconds (105simulation ticks). The original 17drawable sequence entries plus the idle frame are distributed evenly over that cycle, with5/6tick holds. Idle is the final timed slot within the105ticks, before another shot or a queued weapon change can start. The last-shell six-entry sequence already ends on idle and uses17/18tick holds without duplicating it. Weapon runtime consumes completion before the next controller admission, avoiding an extra idle tick. Native game_runtime routes only an active alternate shotgun's sprite-animation stage through weapon_runtime_animate; other objects retain the original animation path. Pause freezes the cycle. Queued weapon changes wait for the full animation, and pending quick clicks survive until the next shot. Switching the option Off restores the original animation clock on the next update.

The last-shell sequence22 has no shell insertion and emits no reload cue. Original mode and the105tick shot interval remain unchanged. The original damage routine rejects repeated damage calls on an enemy in the same frame. The alternate blast therefore traces/impacts all14pellets, groups damage per entity and applies each group once through native combat. Per-pellet hit counts are retained. Difficulty/armor rounding and rewards apply to that grouped damage; no entity protection flags are cleared. Original Off behavior and all other weapons retain their previous paths.
