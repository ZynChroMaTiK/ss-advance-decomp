General damage

src/combat_damage.c recovers the complete control flow of 080037F4. include/ssa/combat_damage.h exposes explicit host projections instead of a packed original entity or player record. This is behavioral recovery, not an exact matching candidate.

Ownership and integration

SsaCombatDamageTarget points at a stable entity, canonical signed 16-bit health, entity timer and player inventory. A flag-0x10 target requires the timer and inventory pointers. Inventory supplies the original player-record +C6 and +C4 armor values; it does not implicitly supply the target health pointer. The native player adapter should pass authoritative inventory.health, then synchronize its entity-health mirror, including after a callback failure. Movement flags at player record +BC are distinct from body flags at entity +30; never synchronize those two fields with each other.

SsaCombatDamage explicitly projects the original globals: current damage source and its entity +20 owner/target pointer, optional selected weapon-hit counter, twelve enemy hit/kill counters, player count, local-view index and local player flags, difficulty, god mode, frame counter, hurt-flash countdown, kill credit, selected credited player's kill byte, kill limit and winner. The optional shot counter points at the record selected by 03003F4C; it is not selected from a weapon field guessed from the source entity. Credit comes separately from 03003F40. Original source identity is not inferred from world ownership.

Callbacks represent 5850 multiplayer damage delivery, 8D5C hurt sound and 125E0 reward. They receive the already-mutated target at the exact call site. The reward callback can invoke the recovered reward helper. Negative callback results stop later operations and retain earlier effects. A context-wide updating guard rejects callback reentry, including a nested NULL-target call. Neither callback nor caller may free or change projected storage during a call. No world query, unlink, pool release or group notification occurs here.

The original dereferences a NULL source for a player target with at most one player. Native code rejects that malformed input; it does not manufacture an enemy source. Missing required host pointers also return -1. A NULL target otherwise retains the original no-op behavior.

Recovered behavior

• Multiplayer winner state suppresses damage. Single-player weapon-hit counting

happens before the target's flag-1 immunity gate, so protected hits still count.

• A multiplayer player target with a non-NULL source only sends damage when the

source, or its explicit +20 referent, is a player controlled by the local player. The network amount truncates to 16 bits. This path does not locally change armor or health. A NULL multiplayer source follows local damage instead.

• Single-player player damage records the enemy source type. Direct sources

include CD..D6; indirect sources include CD..D8. God mode zeroes damage. Difficulty 0 halves damage, 1 computes unsigned damage / 3 * 2, and 2 adds unsigned damage / 2. Other difficulty bytes leave it unchanged. Non-player targets instead receive 1.5x on difficulty 0 and damage / 3 * 2 on 2.

• Armor runs C6 before C4. Each nonzero signed halfword subtracts the signed

low halfword of doubled damage, wraps, then halves unsigned damage. Negative remaining armor refunds its signed division by two and is cleared. Negative input armor and large unsigned damage therefore have deliberately unusual results. No saturation is substituted for original wrapping.

• Player timer becomes 30 in single-player. A local player without record flag 8

gets hurt flash 9 and sound 15 + (frame_counter & 1) only for nonzero remaining damage and an initially zero flash timer. This precedes health subtraction.

• Health subtraction wraps to signed 16 bits. A result at or below zero becomes

zero, increments CD..D8 kill statistics and applies nonzero kill credit. Multiplayer kill bytes wrap; equality with the kill limit sets the winner. Single-player credit calls the reward boundary. Credited dead player bodies get timer 3000 and flag 1 after that boundary.

• After local damage, single-player sets body flag 1 even for survivors. Every

completed local-damage path clears flag 0x800. Early immunity, network and winner exits do not clear it. The enemy controller remains responsible for subsequent death transitions and corpse handling.

Validation

Run .venv-linux/bin/python tests/test_combat_damage_rom.py under WSL, or the workspace test-combat-damage driver once registered. The test compiles native C with strict warnings and UBSan, executes entire original 037F4 including the original unsigned division helper, and controls only the three explicit effect boundaries. It compares full target, game, player-record and settings byte snapshots plus ordered effect snapshots.

The final suite passes 13,186 ROM comparisons, 6,586 callback reentry checks, 10 invalid-input guards and 3 callback failure cases. It covers all scaling branches, 32-bit damage and 16-bit health/armor boundaries, wrapped statistics, direct/indirect source types, multiplayer routing, local protection, god mode, winner gates, NULL targets and credited deaths. There are 232 network, 1,052 sound and 2,009 reward observations. Evidence and source hash are in native-combat-damage-validation.json. Unicorn is only a differential oracle; the native function contains no ROM execution.
