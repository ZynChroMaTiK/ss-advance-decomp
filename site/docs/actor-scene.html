Connected native actor rendering

SsaActorScene composes actor collection/projection, wall clip binding, mixed depth ordering and actual indexed sprite sampling after each fresh SsaWorldScene geometry draw. It owns queue/projection scratch and borrows the geometry, world, actor assets and math tables at stable addresses. Its camera pointer is borrowed only during a draw and cleared on success or failure.

The collector visits visible sectors in ascending order and follows the actual world sector lists. Each visited stable body needs one source snapshot carrying animation, displayed frame, facing, owner and flags. Missing/duplicate sources are errors. The pass mutates geometry's mixed links, so run it once after fresh geometry, rather than twice on the same wall list. On late failure, earlier queue/pixel effects remain; the update guards and camera borrow are cleared.

Wall clips come from actual BE60 results. Closed clips use the original121-byte windows, including the adjacent byte that the original inclusive endpoint can read. Dynamic clips borrow the remaining stable opening allocation rather than fabricating a byte beyond the copied span. Original scratch overlap behavior is preserved within explicit bounded native storage. The framebuffer retains the120-byte predecessor row required by world columns; actor rasterization uses the actual14400 pixel area without an extra hidden row.

The viewer exposes Sam by clearing80 only in his source snapshot when the H chase pose is usable. His canonical flags remain unchanged. D338's second pass draws the original player overlay, and recovered65/66 period4 animations follow the idle/walking predicates. This is an explicit PC rendering extension to the original first-person game. It does not alter gameplay aim or collision.

These actor/world pixel tests establish rendering behavior. They do not establish complete game startup, enemy AI, combat, pickups, HUD, audio or menus. The executable remains a diagnostic native viewer until those stages are implemented and integrated.
