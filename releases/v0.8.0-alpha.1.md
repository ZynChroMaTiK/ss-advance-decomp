# Serious Sam Advance v0.8.0 Alpha 1

Play Serious Sam Advance on Windows with keyboard and mouse controls and all
twelve campaign levels. This is an early release: expect bugs and unfinished features.

## Play

**You need your own ROM of the European version of Serious Sam Advance.**
A ROM is a file containing the original GBA game. Other regional versions are
not supported, and no ROM is included.

1. Download `ss-advance-v0.8.0-alpha.1-windows-x64.zip` and extract it to a folder such as Games.
2. Rename your European ROM to `SSA.gba` and place it in the `orig` folder.
3. Double-click `play-native.cmd`. You do not need build tools or an emulator.

Keep `save.dat` and `settings.dat` when updating to retain your unlocks and settings.
Extra music files are optional. If you need to check your ROM, its SHA-1 fingerprint
should be `4ff48d8c18b70a5184758f1917343ae85a70858e`.

## Included

- All twelve levels across Egypt and Rome, with saved unlocks and original passwords.
- The original enemies, weapons, pickups, traps and encounters.
- Mouse look, optional jumping and a third-person view.
- A picture that fits your window, or the original GBA-resolution view.
- NETRICSA briefings and in-game guide, chapter screens, ending and pause menus.
- End-of-level time and ammo bonuses, with your score carried into the next level.
- Original music and sound, optional SoundFont and MIDI music, and separate volumes.
- Optional dual Colts with reloads and a different double-shotgun spread.

SoundFont is an optional instrument file that changes some of the music's sound.
See `soundfonts/README.md` in the download for setup. Music choices appear in
Settings only when they are available on your computer.

## Controls

WASD moves; mouse looks; left-click fires; E interacts; Space jumps; H switches
view. Number keys 1–0 / mouse wheel select weapons; Q recalls the previous weapon.
Tab/N opens NETRICSA; Esc/P pauses; R restarts the level. In Extras, you can turn
jumping, third-person view and in-game NETRICSA on or off. Alternate weapons starts off.

## Known limitations

- High-score lists, name entry and multiplayer are still unfinished.
- We are still improving the sound, menus and other differences from the original.
- All twelve levels are available for testing. Earlier checks finished eleven in
  a row and the final level separately; the whole campaign still needs more playtesting.
- Saves remember unlocks and settings. They do not save your place halfway through a level.

The game leaves your ROM unchanged. You can also explore the maps in your browser
without a ROM, using the project website below.
